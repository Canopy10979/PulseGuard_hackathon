What Safeguard can—and cannot—do.

A plain-language account of the prototype’s logic, evidence, privacy safeguards, and unfinished work.

Honest status

What works today

  • Real accelerometer and orientation reading on a phone.
  • Recovery and stillness checks before the motion-triggered check-in.
  • Five seconds to respond, followed by ten seconds of warning sound.
  • Browser position, with accuracy shown in metres.
  • A message written for a contact you saved, opened in your own app.
  • Saved contacts stay in this browser. Maps and AED searches use third-party services.
  • No breathing or pulse sensing. This does not detect an overdose.
  • No automatic call to 911 and no responder network.
  • Dashboard figures are illustrative placeholders, not measurements.

Detection

How the rule works

A sudden movement above 2.5 g begins a possible drop. After a short settling period, we check for recovery and then eight seconds of stillness. Resumed movement cancels the pending event. A five-second check-in lets you answer before ten seconds of warning sound. The displayed points describe recorded signals; they are not a medical probability.

Thresholds live at the top of app.js as named constants: IMPACT_MS2, STILL_MS2, STILL_MS, COUNTDOWN_S and MIN_SIGNALS. The browser thresholds are adjustable defaults. The attached mobile screen did not include its settings file, and these values have not been clinically validated.

Data

Sources

The tracker uses your browser’s current location and OpenStreetMap AED records. It lists up to five mapped AEDs within 10 km, closest first by straight-line distance. Access and availability may change. Satellite imagery comes from Esri and is not live footage. The older dashboard figures and risk zones remain illustrative.

Privacy

What we hold and where

The contact name and number stay in the browser storage of the device, under the key pulseguard.contact.v1. Starting location access keeps a watch open until you stop it or leave the page. The marker and accuracy circle show the latest reported fix. Coordinates are sent to the AED lookup service, and map tiles are requested from the imagery provider. Direction links open Google Maps. Safeguard does not upload your saved contact to these services.
