Scoring

How a zone is ranked

Each call adds points. A call where naloxone was given, or where the patient did not recover on scene, counts for more, because those areas need a faster response and more supply, not only more attention.

  • Response call — 1 point
  • Naloxone given — 3 points
  • Transported to hospital — 5 points
  • Died on scene — 12 points

These weights are a choice by the team, not an official standard. Change them in WEIGHT at the top of the script and the ranking changes with them.

Privacy

Two rules we do not break

First, a zone is 500 m across, so a point on the map is a neighbourhood and not a doorway. Second, a zone with fewer than five calls is dropped from the map and the table, because a small count in a small area can point at one household.
