Why it fired

Recorded signals

A possible impact, recovery check and eight seconds of stillness lead to a five-second check-in. The points describe recorded signals, not a medical probability.

    How it works

    From movement to help

    01

    Detect
    A sudden movement above 2.5 g starts the check.

    02

    Confirm
    Movement settles, recovery is checked, then eight seconds of stillness confirms the check-in.

    03

    Ask
    A five-second countdown gives you time to answer.

    04

    Prepare
    No answer starts ten seconds of sound and prepares a message for your contact.
