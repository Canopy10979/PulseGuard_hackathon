Prototype

Lifeline checks in before preparing an alert.

If your phone notices a sudden movement followed by stillness, Lifeline asks a simple question: are you okay? Only then can it prepare a message for someone you trust.

This is a hackathon prototype, not a medical device. It does not detect an overdose, it does not monitor breathing, and it never calls 911. A person must read the prepared message and act. In an emergency, call 911 first.

Response plan

Who gets the message

The message goes to one person you choose. It is written for you and opened in your own messaging app; Lifeline has no server and sends nothing by itself.

Why it fired

Explainable confidence

Two signals are needed before the countdown starts. One signal alone could be a dropped phone, so it never raises an alarm. Each signal is listed with the points it adds.

    How it works

    From movement to help

    01

    Detect
    A sudden movement above 2.5 g starts the check.

    02

    Confirm
    A tilt or eight seconds of stillness adds a second signal.

    03

    Ask
    A twenty second countdown gives you time to cancel.

    04

    Prepare
    No answer, so a message with your position is written for your contact.
