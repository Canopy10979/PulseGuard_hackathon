Help for the moment someone cannot ask.

PulseGuard looks for more than one sign of a possible collapse, checks whether the person can respond, and prepares a way to reach human help.

PulseGuard does not diagnose an overdose. This browser demo reads motion and orientation only; it does not monitor breathing, run in the background, or call anyone automatically.

Current prototype sequence

One signal is not enough.

  1. NoticeA sudden movement is detected.
  2. VerifyStillness or a major orientation change adds a second signal.
  3. AskThe phone asks, “Are you okay?” and starts a cancelable countdown.
  4. PrepareIf there is no answer, the demo prepares a message for a trusted contact.

Future breathing, smartwatch, scheduled, and always-on modes are concepts only and are not implemented in this website.

Before driving

If a substance may affect you, leave the keys behind.

Alcohol, cannabis, illegal drugs, and some prescription or over-the-counter medicines can slow judgment, coordination, or reaction time. Plan a sober ride before using an impairing substance.

King County roads: the county's 2024 report counted 110 DUI-involved collisions in the road system it covers, including seven fatal collisions.

Local response: Seattle operates a DUI squad for suspected alcohol- or drug-impaired driving, while Kent's DUI Court combines supervision with substance-use treatment.
